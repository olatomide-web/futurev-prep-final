What is photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to make food from carbon dioxide and water. It takes place mainly in chloroplasts, where chlorophyll absorbs light energy.

KEY POINTS
  • Light provides the energy.
  • Carbon dioxide and water are raw materials.
  • Chlorophyll absorbs light energy.
  • Glucose is produced and oxygen is released.
IN SIMPLE FORM
CO₂ + H₂O → Glucose + O₂

Light energy is captured by chlorophyll to drive the process.
